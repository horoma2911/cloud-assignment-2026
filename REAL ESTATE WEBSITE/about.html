About HOROMA REAL ESTATE

Our Story

Founded in 2018, HOROMA REAL ESTATE has grown to become one of Tanzania's most trusted real estate agencies. We started as a small family business in Dar es Salaam with a vision to make property transactions easier and more transparent for everyone.

Today, we have expanded our operations to cover major cities across Tanzania including Arusha, Dodoma, Mwanza, Zanzibar, and Morogoro. Our team of experienced real estate professionals is committed to providing exceptional service to our clients.

Our Mission

To provide reliable, professional, and personalized real estate services that exceed our clients' expectations, helping them find their dream properties while ensuring transparency and integrity in every transaction.

Our Vision

To be the leading real estate agency in Tanzania, recognized for our expertise, integrity, and commitment to making property ownership accessible to all Tanzanians and international investors.

Our Core Values

  • Integrity: We conduct business with honesty and transparency
  • Excellence: We strive for the highest quality in all our services
  • Customer Focus: Your satisfaction is our top priority
  • Local Expertise: Deep knowledge of Tanzanian property markets
  • Innovation: Using modern technology to serve you better
